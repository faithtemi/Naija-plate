Party Jollof Rice

The version with the slightly smoky bottom — that's not a mistake, that's the point.

45 minprep + cook
6servings
Mediumdifficulty

Ingredients

  • 3 cups long grain parboiled rice
  • 6 fresh tomatoes
  • 3 red bell peppers (tatashe)
  • 2 scotch bonnet peppers (adjust to taste)
  • 2 medium onions
  • 1/3 cup vegetable oil
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 2 seasoning cubes
  • 1 tsp curry powder
  • 1 tsp thyme
  • 2 bay leaves
  • Salt, to taste
  • 2 cups chicken or beef stock

Method

  1. Blend the tomatoes, bell peppers, scotch bonnet, and one onion together until smooth. Set aside.
  2. Heat the oil in a large pot, slice the second onion, and fry until soft and starting to brown.
  3. Add the tomato paste and fry for 3–4 minutes, stirring so it doesn't burn.
  4. Pour in the blended pepper mix and cook down on medium heat for 20–25 minutes until the sauce thickens and the raw tomato smell is gone.
  5. Add the stock, seasoning cubes, curry powder, thyme, bay leaves, and salt. Taste and adjust.
  6. Wash the rice and add it to the pot. Stir once to coat the rice, then reduce heat to low.
  7. Cover tightly (foil under the lid helps trap steam) and cook for 25–30 minutes, checking once to stir gently from the bottom.
  8. Let it sit off the heat, covered, for 10 minutes before serving.
Why does it taste better the next day?

The rice keeps absorbing flavour from the sauce as it sits, and the smoky bottom layer (the "party rice" bit) softens slightly and mixes through when reheated. Reheat with a splash of water or stock so it doesn't dry out.
