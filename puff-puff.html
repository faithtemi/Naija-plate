Puff-Puff

Fluffy, sweet, deep-fried dough balls. Ready in under an hour, mostly waiting for it to rise.

1 hrmostly rising time
20pieces
Easydifficulty

Ingredients

  • 2 cups plain flour
  • 1/2 cup sugar
  • 1 tsp instant yeast
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1/4 tsp nutmeg (optional)
  • 1 1/4 cups warm water
  • Vegetable oil, for frying

Method

  1. Mix the flour, sugar, yeast, salt, and nutmeg together in a large bowl.
  2. Slowly add the warm water, whisking as you go, until you have a smooth, stretchy batter — thicker than pancake batter but pourable.
  3. Cover the bowl with a cloth and leave in a warm spot for 45–60 minutes, until the batter has doubled and looks bubbly.
  4. Heat oil in a deep pot to about 170°C (a drop of batter should sizzle and rise back up right away).
  5. Wet your hand with water, scoop a ball of batter, and squeeze it out between your thumb and index finger into the hot oil.
  6. Fry in small batches, turning occasionally, for 4–5 minutes until deep golden brown all over.
  7. Remove and drain on paper towels. Serve warm, on their own or dusted with a little extra sugar.
Mine came out flat and oily, not fluffy

That's almost always the batter not being given enough time to rise, or the oil not being hot enough when you dropped it in. If the batter didn't visibly bubble and double, give it more time somewhere warmer before frying.
